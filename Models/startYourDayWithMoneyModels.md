If you flip the lens from *"How do I make people use my product?"* to *"How do I use these principles on myself to build wealth?"*, you get a **personal wealth-building system**.

Here is the **Human Behavior Stack for Getting Rich**, using the same psychological models—but applied to **you**.

## 🧠 The Wealth Mindset: From "User" to "Creator"

Most people are on the receiving end of these models (they are the *users*).  
Rich people use these models on **themselves** and on **the market**.

---

### 1. ATTENTION — "Where is your focus?"

**Principle:** What you pay attention to grows.

**Wealth application:**
- **Curiosity Gap:** Instead of consuming entertainment, ask: *"What skill, if mastered, would 10x my income?"*
- **Von Restorff Effect:** Stand out. Be the unusual one in your field—the one with a unique skill combination.
- **Priming:** Start your day with financial education, not social media. Prime your brain for wealth.

> **Action:**  
> Control your attention. If you don't, someone else will (and they'll monetize it).

---

### 2. MOTIVATION — "Why do you want wealth?"

**Principle:** Self-Determination Theory

**Wealth application:**
- **Autonomy:** Wealth = freedom. You're not building money; you're building **choice**.
- **Competence:** Get visibly better at a high-value skill every single day.
- **Relatedness:** Surround yourself with people who are wealthier or more ambitious than you.

> **Action:**  
> Define your "why" clearly. Not "I want money." But: *"I want to never have to ask permission again."*

---

### 3. DECISION — "How do you make smart choices?"

**Principle:** Framing, Anchoring, Loss Aversion, Social Proof

**Wealth application:**
- **Framing:** Frame every expense as *"hours of your life"*—not dollars. A ₹50,000 watch = 200 hours of work.
- **Anchoring:** When negotiating salary or prices, always **anchor high**. The first number sets the range.
- **Loss Aversion:** Use this on yourself. Put money in accounts you can't easily touch. Make losing it *painful*.
- **Social Proof:** Follow the habits of rich people, not the spending of rich people.

> **Action:**  
> Before any big decision, ask: *"Would a wealthy version of me do this?"*

---

### 4. ACTION — "Do you actually do the work?"

**Principle:** Fogg Behavior Model

**Wealth application:**
- **Ability:** Break wealth-building into tiny daily actions.
  - Invest ₹500/day (SIP).
  - Learn 1 new business concept/day.
  - Send 1 sales email/day.
- **Prompt:** Set environmental triggers.
  - Phone reminder: *"Did you learn or earn today?"*
  - Desktop wallpaper: *"Net worth goal: X"*
- **Motivation:** Connect action to identity (see below).

> **Action:**  
> Make wealth-building **easy**. Automate savings, investing, and learning.

---

### 5. HABIT — "Does wealth become automatic?"

**Principle:** Hook Model

**Wealth application:**
- **Trigger:** Daily reminder → Open investment app → Invest → See portfolio grow.
- **Variable Reward:** Check investments. Sometimes up, sometimes down. The uncertainty keeps you engaged.
- **Investment:** Every ₹1 invested today is an investment in future freedom. The more you invest, the more the system works for you.

> **Action:**  
> Build a daily wealth habit. Even 10 minutes. Consistency > intensity.

---

### 6. RETENTION — "Do you stick with it?"

**Principle:** Goal-Gradient, Zeigarnik, Commitment & Consistency, Identity

**Wealth application:**
- **Goal-Gradient:** Set clear wealth milestones.
  - ₹10L → ₹50L → ₹1Cr → ₹5Cr.
  - Each milestone feels closer and motivates more.
- **Zeigarnik:** Don't finish your monthly budget too early—leave it open so it stays in your mind.
- **Commitment:** Tell someone your wealth goal. Public commitment increases follow-through.
- **Identity:** This is the big one.
  - Not: *"I want to be rich."*
  - But: **"I am an investor."**  
    **"I am a business builder."**  
    **"I am someone who creates value."**

> **Action:**  
> Change your internal narrative. Say: *"I'm not trying to get rich—I am becoming a wealthy person."*

---

### 7. BUSINESS — "How do you turn behavior into wealth?"

**Principle:** Jobs-to-be-Done, Reciprocity, Authority Bias, Gamification

**Wealth application:**
- **Jobs-to-be-Done:** Don't sell products. Sell **outcomes**.
  - Not "course" → "career change."
  - Not "tool" → "time saved."
- **Reciprocity:** Give massive value for free. Content, tools, advice. People will pay later.
- **Authority Bias:** Build your reputation. Become the expert. Credibility = premium pricing.
- **Gamification:** Turn your business into a game.
  - Revenue goals = levels.
  - Clients = XP.
  - New skills = power-ups.

> **Action:**  
> Build something that creates value for others. Wealth is a byproduct of value delivered.
