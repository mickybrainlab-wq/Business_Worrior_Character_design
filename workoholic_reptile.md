### LEVEL 1: The "Hunt" (Immediate Visual Feedback)

**The Hack:** The Reptile is a predator. Predators are addicted to **"The Chase."** 

- **Signal:** Every 25 minutes, you must physically stand up, stretch your arms wide (like a hawk spreading wings), and physically point your finger at the single most important task you just completed.

- **The Reptile Feeling:** *"I see the dot. Dot = Kill. More dots = More kills."* 
- **The Hook (Gap):** *Can I get 5 red dots before the sun moves to that spot on my wall?*

### LEVEL 2: The "Territory" (Ownership & Status)
**The Problem:** If the work doesn't belong to you, the Reptile sees it as "slave labor" and resists it.
**The Hack:** The Reptile is fiercely territorial. It will guard its cave with its life. 

- **Signal:** Rename your work. It is not "The Project." It is **"My Kingdom."** 
- **Instruction:** Every morning, take 2 minutes to physically walk around your workspace. Touch your keyboard, touch your chair, touch your notebooks. Say out loud (whisper if you have to): *"This is my ground. I own the output here."* 
- **The Reptile Feeling:** *"Intruders (distractions) are trying to enter my territory. I must destroy them to protect my status."*
- **The Hook (Gap):** *If I finish this specific chunk today, my territory expands. How much bigger is my Kingdom at 5 PM compared to 9 AM?*

### LEVEL 3: The "2-Variable Dopamine Slot" (The Flow State)
**The Problem:** You try to think about the deadline, the quality, the boss's opinion, AND the task all at once. That is 4 variables. The Reptile crashes.
**The Hack:** The Reptile can only process **Danger** vs **Reward** at the same time. 

- **Signal:** Reduce every single work session to just TWO physical sensations:
    - **Variable 1:** The feeling of your fingers moving (typing/writing).
    - **Variable 2:** The feeling of the clock ticking (Time pressure).
- **Instruction:** Set a countdown timer for 45 minutes. You are NOT allowed to think about "finishing." You are ONLY allowed to think: *"Can I make my fingers move faster than the clock?"* 
- **The Reptile Feeling:** *"Clock is the predator. My fingers are the escape. Move faster to survive."* This triggers a mild adrenaline rush, which is addictive.
- **The Hook (Gap):** *The clock resets in 45 mins. I beat it last time. Can I beat it again right now?*

### LEVEL 4: The "Irrelevant Work" Hack (Micro-Wins)

- **Signal:** Break the boring work into chunks so small that they are stupid. E.g., "Open the email app." "Read the first email." "Type 'Ok'." 
- **Instruction:** Every time you complete one of these micro-chunks, you must physically **snap your fingers** or **tap the desk twice**. This is a "Reward Click" for the Reptile. 
- **The Reptile Feeling:** *"Snap = Dopamine. More snaps = More dopamine. I don't care if it's relevant; I just want the snap."*
- **The Hook (Gap):** *I got 10 snaps from boring emails. How many snaps can I get from the hard work? Maybe 50 snaps? Let's do the hard work to get more snaps.*
